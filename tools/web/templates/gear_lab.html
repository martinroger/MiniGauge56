🧠 Model Theory & Assumptions ▶ Expand
M1: Gated Heuristic
• Assumptions: Rigid mechanical coupling; discrete step shifts; zero ratio phase lag.
• Mechanics: Physical gating (speed≥cutoff, RPM≥cutoff, |Δr/Δt|≤0.05), ratio EMA (α), constant tolerance window (±tolabs) with Voronoi collision protection, and temporal latching debounce.
M2: Kinematic Bayes
• Assumptions: Dynamic context vector ut = [V, ΔV/Δt, RPM, ΔRPM/Δt]; signed acceleration transition conditioning; loss-of-fix prediction.
• Mechanics: Conditioned transition prior Tij(ut). When ratio leaves corridor or engine drops during shift (loss of fix), predicts probable target gears (accelerating in 4th → 5th > 4th > 3rd; braking → downshifts) with synchronous RPM likelihood and guarded latching.
M3: HMM State-Space
• Assumptions: First-order Markov chain with empirical shift matrix A6×6; self-inertia (~97%); asymmetric engine braking physics.
• Mechanics: Forward Viterbi belief update with directional clutch-drop suppression (ΔfRPM < cutoff): upward gear emissions are suppressed during coast-down engine deceleration, eliminating phantom upshifts.
